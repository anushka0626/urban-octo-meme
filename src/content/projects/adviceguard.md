## The problem

An advisor can't re-read every client's portfolio before each decision, so the clients who have drifted furthest from their advice are easy to miss.

## What it does

- **Client triage:** ranks a demo book of about 220 investors by how far each has drifted from their advice, and explains the gap.
- **Meeting intelligence:** transcribes a client meeting with Whisper and extracts what was discussed.
- **Advisor approval:** turns those conversations into suggested actions that an advisor reviews and approves.
- **Runs offline:** the language model and speech-to-text run locally, so client conversations never leave the machine.
