## What it is

MOS models a simplified CPU and memory so that low-level execution can be followed step by step.

## What I worked on

I implemented command-driven operations, including `GD`, `PD`, `SR` and `LR`, which are used to interact with and control the simulated system.

## What I learned

Working through a simplified model gave me a hands-on feel for how instructions and memory operations actually execute.
